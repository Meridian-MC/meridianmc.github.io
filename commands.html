Commands

The commands you'll reach for most often. Arguments in <angle brackets> are required; [square brackets] are optional. Some commands depend on your rank or your permissions inside a land.

Getting around

/spawn
Return to the server spawn.
/rtp
Random-teleport out into unclaimed wilderness.
/back
Return to your last location, including your death point.
/lands spawn
Teleport to your land's spawn point.

Homes & personal

/sethome [name]
Set a home where you're standing. Your home limit depends on your rank.
/home [name]
Teleport to a home. /homes lists them.
/delhome <name>
Delete a home.
/afk
Toggle your AFK status.
/rules /motd
Show the rules / message of the day in chat.

Communication

/msg <player> <text>
Private message. /r <text> replies to the last one.
/mail send <player> <text>
Leave a message for an offline player. /mail read to check yours.
/lands chat
Toggle land-only chat (also /lc).
/ignore <player>
Stop seeing a player's messages.

Economy & shops

/balance [player]
Check a balance (also /bal).
/pay <player> <amount>
Send money to another player.
/baltop
The richest players on the server.
/shop
Open the server shop (basic commodities only, high prices).
Chest shops — QuickShop-Hikari
left-click a chest
While holding an item, left-click a chest to start a shop, then type a price.
/qs price <amount>
Change the price of the shop you're looking at.
/qs find <item>
Locate nearby shops selling an item.
/qs staff add <player>
Let someone help manage your shop.
/qs remove
Remove the shop you're looking at.
/trade <player>
Open a secure face-to-face trade window.

Jobs

/jobs browse
See the available jobs and join one.
/jobs join <job> /jobs leave <job>
Join or quit a job.
/jobs stats
Your level and progress in each job you hold.
/jobs info <job>
What a job pays for.
/jobs top <job>
Leaderboard for a job.

Land & nation

/lands menu
Open the main Lands GUI — everything below is in here too.
/lands create <name>
Found a land. The first chunk is free.
/lands claim /lands unclaim
Claim or release the chunk you're standing in.
/lands map
Show a chunk map of nearby claims.
/lands view
Visualize your land's borders.
/lands trust <player> /lands untrust <player>
Add or remove a trusted member.
/lands info
Tier, chunk count, members, and bank.
/lands bank deposit <amount>
Fund the land bank (also withdraw, balance).
/lands rent /lands sell
Rent or sell a sub-area to another player.
/lands setspawn
Set where /lands spawn takes members.
/nation create <name>
Form a nation from your land ($7,500,000).
/nation <nation> invite <land>
Invite another land into your nation.

Warfare

War declarations must be approved by staff in a Discord ticket first. Only then do you file it in-game. See Warfare.
/nation <your_nation> war declare <target>
File an approved declaration. Starts the 2-day grace period.
/nation <nation> relations
Check standing wars, alliances, and truces.

Command names follow the plugins Meridian runs (Lands, QuickShop-Hikari, EssentialsX, Jobs Reborn, TradeManager, EzRTP). If any alias differs on the server, tell staff and this page will be corrected.
