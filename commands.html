Commands

The commands you'll reach for most often. Arguments in <angle brackets> are required; [square brackets] are optional. Some commands depend on your rank or your permissions inside a land.

Getting around

/spawnReturn to the server spawn.
/rtpRandom-teleport out into unclaimed wilderness (also /wild).
/tpa <player>Ask to teleport to a player.
/tpahere <player>Ask a player to teleport to you.
/tpaccept · /tpdenyAnswer a pending teleport request.
/backReturn to your last location, including your death point.
/lands spawnTeleport to your land's spawn point.

Homes & personal

/sethome [name]Set a home where you're standing. Your home limit depends on your rank.
/home [name]Teleport to a home. /homes lists them.
/delhome <name>Delete a home.
/afkToggle your AFK status.
/rules · /motdShow the rules / message of the day in chat.

Communication

/msg <player> <text>Private message. /r <text> replies to the last one.
/mail send <player> <text>Leave a message for an offline player. /mail read to check yours.
/lands chatToggle land-only chat (also /lc).
/ignore <player>Stop seeing a player's messages.

Economy & shops

/balance [player]Check a balance (also /bal).
/pay <player> <amount>Send money to another player.
/baltopThe richest players on the server.
/shopOpen the server shop (basic commodities only, high prices).
Chest shops — QuickShop-Hikari
left-click a chestWhile holding an item, left-click a chest to start a shop, then type a price.
/qs price <amount>Change the price of the shop you're looking at.
/qs find <item>Locate nearby shops selling an item.
/qs staff add <player>Let someone help manage your shop.
/qs removeRemove the shop you're looking at.
/trade <player>Open a secure face-to-face trade window.

Jobs

/jobs browseSee the available jobs and join one.
/jobs join <job> · /jobs leave <job>Join or quit a job.
/jobs statsYour level and progress in each job you hold.
/jobs info <job>What a job pays for.
/jobs top <job>Leaderboard for a job.

Land & nation

/lands menuOpen the main Lands GUI — everything below is in here too.
/lands create <name>Found a land. First chunk is free.
/lands claim · /lands unclaimClaim or release the chunk you're standing in.
/lands mapShow a chunk map of nearby claims.
/lands viewVisualize your land's borders.
/lands trust <player> · /lands untrust <player>Add or remove a trusted member.
/lands infoTier, chunk count, members, and bank.
/lands bank deposit <amount>Fund the land bank (also withdraw, balance).
/lands rent · /lands sellRent or sell a sub-area to another player.
/lands setspawnSet where /lands spawn takes members.
/nation create <name>Form a nation from your land ($75,000).
/nation <nation> invite <land>Invite another land into your nation.

Warfare

War declarations must be approved by staff in a Discord ticket first. Only then do you file it in-game. See Warfare.
/nation <your_nation> war declare <target>File an approved declaration. Starts the 2-day grace period.
/nation <nation> relationsCheck standing wars, alliances, and truces.

Command names follow the plugins Meridian runs (Lands, QuickShop-Hikari, EssentialsX, Jobs Reborn, TradeManager, EzRTP). If any alias differs on the server, tell staff and this page will be corrected.
