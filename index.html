The systems that run Meridian

Meridian is defined by a handful of plugins. Know these and you know how the server plays.

Lands QuickShop-Hikari EconomyShopGUI Jobs Reborn The Brewing Project EssentialsX Grief protection

Lands

v8.2.4

The backbone of the server. Claim land chunk by chunk, form a land with a shared bank, then combine lands into nations with their own taxes, tiers, and tracked diplomacy.

Claims are protected around the clock — the only time that changes is a staff-approved war.

Lands & Nations guide →

QuickShop-Hikari

v6.3.0.1

Player-to-player chest shops. Left-click a chest holding the item you want to trade, set a price, and you have a store. These shops are the Meridian economy.

Economy guide →

EconomyShopGUI

v7.2.1

The server shop — deliberately limited to basic commodities like torches and bread, at deliberately high prices. There is no sell-to-server option. It exists to keep a real free market, not to be your income.

Economy guide →

Jobs Reborn

v5.2.6.6

Five jobs — Miner, Farmer, Fisherman, Hunter, Explorer — that pay small amounts for relevant actions. Daily earnings cap at $15; it is starter capital, not a salary. Run /jobs browse to sign up.

Economy guide →

The Brewing Project

v3.3.3

Overhauled, recipe-driven brewing. Ferment ingredients in barrels, distill with cauldrons, age for quality — the result depends on getting every step right. A trade good in its own right.

EssentialsX

v2.22.1

The everyday commands: homes, /tpa, /pay, /balance, mail, kits, and chat. Backed by Vault, which links the currency to every other plugin on this list.

Command reference →

CoreProtect & GrimAC

CoreProtect 25.0 · Grim 2.3.74

CoreProtect logs every block change, so griefing inside a claim can be rolled back and traced. GrimAC handles anticheat — movement, combat, and packet checks — alongside NoChatReports for chat.
