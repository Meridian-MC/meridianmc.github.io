How the Meridian Economic Index is calculated

The index reads the server's economy log (every balance change), the land databases, and the shop transaction history. It needs at least 8 active traders and 14 days of history before it will assign a state. Each week it compares the last 7 days of trade volume against the 7 days before that:

Expansion
Trade volume up more than 10% week over week.
Steady
Trade volume within a −5% to +10% band. A healthy, consistent market.
Cooling
Trade volume down 5% to 25%. Activity is slowing.
Recession
Trade volume down more than 25%, week over week.
Insufficient data
Not enough players or history to judge.

The Gini figure measures how concentrated wealth is: 0 means everyone holds the same, 1 means one player holds everything. The price index tracks a fixed basket of wheat, iron, diamonds, ender pearls and oak logs against player-shop prices, set to 100 at launch.
