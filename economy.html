Economy Guide

Meridian runs a player-driven economy. Almost every item in circulation was made or gathered by someone. Here's how money works and how to start earning.

1. The Currency

  • The currency is the Meridian Mark (⧗). Check your balance with /balance.
  • Send money to another player with /pay <player> <amount>.
  • There is no money printer — the economy only grows through server-shop sales and small job payouts, so prices stay meaningful.
  • New players start with a small stipend to get going.

2. Ways to Earn

  • Sell to other players. Set up a shop (below) and stock what people actually need — food, building blocks, enchanted gear, potions.
  • Jobs. Join a job with /jobs join <name> (mining, farming, hunting, etc.) to earn a small payout per relevant action.
  • Sell raw goods to the server shop for a guaranteed but lower price — this is the price floor.
  • Services. Enchanting, farm building, grinding XP, mercenary work during wars, transport.
  • Voting. Vote for the server daily for Marks and crate keys.

3. Player Shops

  • Place a chest, then /shop create <buy_price> <sell_price> while looking at it, holding the item you want to trade.
  • Other players right-click the sign to buy, shift-right-click to sell to you.
  • Shops only work inside a claim where you have permission — protect your shop chunk.
  • Undercutting is allowed and encouraged. That's the market working.

4. Server Shop & Market

  • /shop or the market NPC at spawn opens the admin shop — fixed buy/sell prices for common raw materials.
  • Server-shop prices are deliberately unattractive for buying. It exists as a safety net, not a convenience.
  • Some items are sell-only or buy-only to protect the player market.

5. Nation Economy

  • Each nation has a shared bank that pays claim upkeep. Fund it with /nation deposit <amount>.
  • Nations can set a tax that collects a small amount from members daily: /nation tax <amount>.
  • Nations can trade land, sign treaties with reparations, and run collective shops at their capital.

6. Safe Trading

Scamming through shops (mispriced signs) is allowed — check the numbers. Scamming through trust (taking a deposit and not delivering, "hold this for me", fake middlemen) is a rules violation and will be punished.
  • For big deals, use a shop chest or an admin as a middleman — never hand items over on a promise.
  • Screenshot or record large trades and agreements.
  • Treaty payments must go through /pay or the nation bank so there's a log.

7. Command Reference

  • /balance / /bal <player> — check balance
  • /pay <player> <amount> — send Marks
  • /shop create <buy> <sell> — make a shop chest
  • /shop — open the server market
  • /jobs browse / /jobs join <name> — jobs
  • /vote — vote links and rewards
  • /baltop — richest players
