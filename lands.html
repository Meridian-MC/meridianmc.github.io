Lands Guide

Land is how you stake a permanent claim on Meridian. A claim protects your builds and chests from everyone except during a declared war. Here's how to get started.

1. The Basics

  • The world is divided into chunks (16×16 blocks). You claim land one chunk at a time.
  • Inside your claim, other players cannot break/place blocks, open containers, or use your stuff unless you grant them permission.
  • Claims are protected at all times except during a war your nation is part of — see War Rules.
  • Unclaimed wilderness has no protection. Build there at your own risk.

2. Your First Claim

  1. Find a spot at least a few hundred blocks from spawn and from other nations' borders.
  2. Create your nation: /nation create <name>. This also claims the chunk you're standing in as your capital.
  3. Set your home: /nation sethome — this is where /nation home teleports members.
  4. Claim adjacent chunks by standing in them and running /claim.
New nations get a 7-day protection window where no one can declare war on you. Use it to get established.

3. Nations & Members

  • Invite players with /nation invite <player>; they join with /nation join <name>.
  • Your maximum claimable chunks scale with member count (e.g. 12 chunks per member — check /nation info for current values).
  • A nation with 0 online members for an extended period may become raidable or fall to decay. Keep it active.

4. Expanding

  • All claims must be connected to your capital — no island chunks scattered across the map.
  • You cannot claim within 3 chunks of another nation's border without their permission.
  • Outposts (detached claims) require a separate /nation outpost and cost more to maintain.

5. Permissions & Roles

  • Default ranks: Leader → Officer → Member → Ally → Outsider.
  • Set what each rank can do with /nation perm <rank> <build|container|interact> <true|false>.
  • Grant a single trusted player access to a specific chunk with /claim trust <player>.
  • Mark a chunk as public (e.g. a shop plaza) with /claim flag public true.

6. Upkeep & Decay

  • Each claimed chunk costs a small daily upkeep from your nation bank.
  • If the bank hits zero, claims begin to decay (oldest/outermost first) until upkeep is affordable again.
  • Deposit with /nation deposit <amount>. Check status with /nation bank.

7. Land in Wartime

  • During a declared war, enemies can break blocks and open chests inside your claims.
  • Territory only changes hands through a successful siege (see War Rules §4).
  • When the war ends, all raid permissions are revoked and normal protection resumes.

8. Command Reference

  • /nation create <name> — found a nation
  • /claim / /unclaim — claim or release the current chunk
  • /nation map — show nearby claims
  • /nation info [name] — stats, limits, bank
  • /nation invite <player> / /nation kick <player>
  • /nation sethome / /nation home
  • /nation deposit <amt> / /nation withdraw <amt>
