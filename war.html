War Rules

War is the only time claimed land can be raided and members can be freely killed. These rules keep it a contest instead of a mess. Breaking them voids your war claims and can end in a ban.

1. Declaring War

  • Only a nation leader or an appointed officer may declare war.
  • Declarations must be posted in the #war-declarations channel and name: the attacking nation, the defending nation, the reason (casus belli), and the war goal.
  • A war becomes active 24 hours after the declaration is posted. No hostile action before then.
  • You may not declare war on a nation more than 2x your total claim size or member count without their consent.

2. Grace Period & Truces

  • New nations have a 7-day protection window from creation during which they cannot be declared on.
  • After a war ends, both sides get a 72-hour mandatory truce before a new war between them can start.
  • Negotiated peace treaties (payments, land transfer, non-aggression pacts) are binding once posted in #treaties. Breaking a treaty is a bannable offence.

3. Combat Rules

  • During an active war, members of both nations can be killed anywhere except protected spawn zones.
  • Full loot on death applies during war. Keep-inventory perks do not function in war zones.
  • No combat logging. Disconnecting during combat forfeits your inventory to the other side if reported with evidence.
  • Spawn-camping the enemy's respawn point is not allowed. Give them a reasonable chance to leave spawn.
  • Alliances must be declared publicly. No secret third-party interference.

4. Sieges & Objectives

  • To capture territory you must break the defender's war banner / obsidian core inside their claim and hold the area for the siege timer (default 10 minutes).
  • Only the land immediately connected to a captured core can be claimed as spoils — you cannot teleport-claim across the map.
  • Defenders may rebuild and re-place a core between sieges.
  • TNT and cannons are allowed against claimed structures only during an active war.

5. Spoils & Raiding

  • During war you may open, break, and loot chests and blocks inside the enemy's claims.
  • You may not permanently pave, lava-cast, or cobble-monster an enemy's build to make it unusable. Loot it, don't ruin it.
  • Captured land and agreed reparations are the only permanent outcomes. Everything else resets to peace rules when the war ends.

6. Ending a War

  • A war ends when: the war goal is met, one side surrenders in #treaties, both sides agree to white peace, or 14 days pass with no siege activity (auto-timeout).
  • On war end, all temporary raid permissions are revoked immediately.

7. Forbidden Tactics

  • No lag machines, crash exploits, or client hacks — the server rules still apply in war.
  • No griefing unclaimed spawn infrastructure, shops, or public warps.
  • No real-money payments for war participation or outcomes.
  • No using alts to scout, hold ground, or pad numbers.
  • No withholding a declared war goal to grief a nation indefinitely.
