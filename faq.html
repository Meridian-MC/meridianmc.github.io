Getting started

How do I join?

Java Edition, address meridian-mc.net. Clients from 1.8 through 26.2 are supported through ViaVersion and ViaBackwards.

What should I do first?

Get some starting cash from a job, then found a land with /lands create <name> and read the Lands guide. The Commands page has everything else.

Economy

What's the currency?

A single currency, written $, not tied to any physical item. Everyone starts with $2,500. Check your balance with /balance and send money with /pay <player> <amount>.

Is there a server shop?

Yes, but it only sells a handful of basic commodities like torches and bread, and it is buy-only. You cannot sell anything to the server for cash. This keeps the market in players' hands.

Why is the server shop so expensive?

It is priced as an emergency option, not a convenience. It should always be cheaper to buy from another player, so the high prices push you toward the chest-shop market.

How do I make money?

Mainly by trading with other players. Chest shops are the real economy: left-click a chest with the item you want to sell to set one up, find what is in demand, produce it, and sell it. There is no single get-rich-quick method.

Are there jobs I can do for money?

Yes. Miner, Farmer, Fisherman, Hunter, and Explorer pay out small amounts for relevant actions. Jobs are a side hustle for early capital, not a reliable income. Daily earnings are capped at $1,500, and casual play earns well under that. Run /jobs browse to sign up.

How should I price my items?

Players set their own prices. There is no fixed price list for anything traded player-to-player. Check a few shops, see what people are asking, and price accordingly. The Economy page tracks a price index for common goods.

What about scamming?

Scamming through a mispriced shop sign is on the buyer to catch. Scamming through trust, meaning taking a payment and not delivering, fake middlemen, or “hold this for me”, is a Rulebook violation. For big deals, use a chest shop or ask staff to middleman.

Are villager trades limited?

A few are, to keep the player market fair:

  • Librarian re-rolling is disabled. You cannot break and replace a lectern to fish for a specific enchanted book.
  • Villagers do not sell diamond gear. No diamond armour, swords, axes, pickaxes or shovels from armorers, weaponsmiths or toolsmiths. Craft your own or buy from a player.
  • Clerics do not sell ender pearls, and farmers do not buy crops for emeralds. Both are protected tradeable commodities, the same way automated farms for them are banned under the Farms rule.

Everything else works as it does in vanilla.

The world

How big is the map?

The overworld is 10,000 × 10,000 blocks.

Is the End open?

No.

Why is the Nether so small?

The overworld is 10,000 × 10,000, and the Nether runs at an 8:1 ratio to it, so the Nether is small. That makes netherite and other Nether loot genuinely scarce. To keep it from being permanently strip-mined, the Nether is wiped every month.

When does the Nether open?

Not yet. It opens on a fixed date after launch, announced server-wide when it happens.
