The world

How the map, the dimensions, and combat are set up.

How big is the map?

The overworld is 10,000 × 10,000 blocks, centred on spawn, so the furthest border is 5,000 blocks out in any direction. The border is solid; you cannot build past it.

Is the End open?

No. The End is closed.

Why is the Nether so small, and why does it get wiped?

The Nether runs at Minecraft's normal 8:1 ratio to the overworld, so a 10,000-wide overworld gives a Nether only about 1,250 blocks wide. That makes netherite, blaze rods, and other Nether loot genuinely scarce and worth trading.

To stop that small space from being permanently strip-mined and claimed, the Nether resets on the first of every month. Anything you leave in the Nether is gone at the reset, so treat it as a place you raid, not a place you live.

When does the Nether open?

Not at launch. It opens on a fixed date afterward, announced server-wide when it happens.

Is PvP on?

PvP is off for the opening period so people can settle in and get established. It is switched on server-wide once nations have formed, and the change is announced in advance. Once it is on, PvP is allowed everywhere outside declared wars, subject to the PvP rules.

What difficulty is the server?

Normal survival. It is not hardcore, and there is no keep-inventory outside of war, so a death in the wild means dropping your things where you fell.

Can I fly?

Only inside land you are trusted in. Land owners can grant flight to their members through the Lands role settings. Everywhere else, flight is off.

Is there anti-cheat and x-ray protection?

Yes. GrimAC handles movement, combat, and packet checks. On top of that, the server hides ores from your client until you are close to them, so x-ray gives you nothing but a ban if you are caught using it.

Getting started

Joining and your first hour.

How do I join?

Java Edition, address meridian-mc.net. Clients from 1.8 through 26.2 are supported through ViaVersion and ViaBackwards. You need a genuine (premium) Minecraft account.

Is there a whitelist?

Before launch the server is whitelisted. From launch day the whitelist comes off and anyone can connect at meridian-mc.net.

What should I do first?

Gather a little starting cash, then found a land with /lands create <name> and read the Lands guide. Sign up for a job for early income, and check the Commands page for everything else.

Economy

Money, the market, and how trading works. The Economy page has the live numbers.

What's the currency?

A single currency, written $, not tied to any physical item. Everyone starts with $2,500. Check your balance with /balance and send money with /pay <player> <amount>.

Is there a server shop?

Yes, but it only sells a handful of basic commodities like torches and bread, and it is buy-only. You cannot sell anything to the server for cash. That keeps the market in players' hands.

Why is the server shop so expensive?

It is priced as an emergency option, not a convenience. It should always be cheaper to buy from another player, so the high prices push you toward the chest-shop market.

How do I make money?

Mostly by trading with other players. Chest shops are the real economy: left-click a chest holding the item you want to sell to set one up, work out what is in demand, produce it, and sell it. There is no single get-rich-quick method.

Are there jobs I can do for money?

Yes. Miner, Farmer, Fisherman, Hunter, and Explorer pay out small amounts for relevant actions. Jobs are a side hustle for early capital, not a living. Daily earnings are capped at $1,500, and casual play earns well under that. Run /jobs browse to sign up.

How should I price my items?

Players set their own prices. There is no fixed price list for anything traded player-to-player. Check a few shops, see what people are charging, and price accordingly. The Economy page tracks the going rate for common goods.

Can I move enchantments between items?

Yes, that is what ExtractableEnchantments is for. Craft an Enchantment Extractor (four experience bottles, four lapis blocks, and a book), then drag it onto an enchanted item to pull one random enchantment off as a book. It always works and you keep the book, so you can strip a good enchantment off gear you are replacing and re-use it or sell it.

Are villager trades limited?

A few are, to keep the player market fair:

  • Librarian re-rolling is disabled. You cannot break and replace a lectern to fish for a specific enchanted book.
  • Villagers do not sell diamond gear. No diamond armour, swords, axes, pickaxes, or shovels from armorers, weaponsmiths, or toolsmiths. Craft your own or buy from a player.
  • Clerics do not sell ender pearls, and farmers do not buy crops for emeralds. Both are protected commodities, the same way automated farms for them are banned under the Farms rule.

Everything else works the way it does in vanilla.

What about scamming?

Scamming through a mispriced shop sign is on the buyer to catch. Scamming through trust, meaning taking a payment and not delivering, fake middlemen, or “hold this for me”, is a Rulebook violation. For big deals, use a chest shop or ask staff to middleman.
