Server Rules

These rules apply to every player on Meridian at all times. Not knowing a rule is not an excuse for breaking it. Staff decisions are final.

1. Player Conduct

  • Treat other players with basic respect. Banter is fine; targeted harassment, hate speech, slurs, and discrimination are not.
  • No threats of real-world harm, doxxing, or sharing anyone's personal information.
  • No sexual content involving minors, ever. This is an instant permanent ban and a report.
  • Do not impersonate staff or other players.

2. Cheating & Exploits

  • No hacked clients, cheat mods, or modifications that give an unfair advantage (kill aura, fly, x-ray, reach, auto-clickers, etc.).
  • Allowed client mods: performance mods (Sodium, Lithium), minimaps without entity/cave radar, Litematica in non-printer mode. If unsure, ask staff first.
  • No abusing bugs or exploits. Report them to staff instead — duping, lag machines, and unintended item generation are bannable.
  • AFK machines and farms are allowed within reason. Anything that noticeably degrades server performance may be removed.

3. Griefing & Stealing

  • Griefing, stealing, and PvP outside of declared war are covered by the Lands and War rules.
  • Unclaimed land is fair game. Claimed land may only be raided during an active, declared war.
  • Do not trap, cage, or hold players against their will outside of war.

4. Chat & Communication

  • Keep global chat English so staff can moderate. Use nation/party channels for other languages.
  • No spam, excessive caps, or chat flooding.
  • No advertising other servers or unrelated services.
  • Keep politics and other heated real-world topics out of global chat.

5. Builds & Land Use

  • No offensive, hateful, or explicit builds, skins, or item names.
  • Do not build directly on another nation's border to grief or box them in.
  • Respect the world border and any staff-marked protected/spawn zones.

6. Accounts & Alts

  • One account per person for voting and reward purposes.
  • Alt accounts may not be used to inflate a nation's claim size, dodge bans, or spy. Ban evasion extends the original punishment.
  • You are responsible for anything done on your account. Secure your login.

7. Enforcement

Punishments scale with severity and history, typically: warning → mute/temp-ban → permanent ban. Serious offences (hacking, chargebacks, hate speech, threats) skip straight to a ban. Appeals go through our Discord.

Questions about a rule? Ask in our Discord before you act, not after.
